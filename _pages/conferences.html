Sources. Impact factors are 2025 JCR values, read from IEEE Xplore (TPAMI, TNNLS, Transactions on Big Data), the IEEE ITS Society (T-ITS), Springer (IJCV) and Elsevier's journal pages. Review times are the publishers' own medians. Elsevier reports decision after review, which excludes desk rejections; its much shorter "first decision" figure is dominated by them. Springer notes that IJCV's 37-day median is computed the same way and that a reviewed manuscript typically takes about 96 days. IEEE does not publish median review times for these titles. A dash means no figure I could verify from the publisher.
